# Client Pitch Kit & Monetization Playbook

## 1. The Core Value Pitch (Preventing Costly No-Shows)
Local businesses lose 15%–25% of their revenue from customers who book an appointment over the phone and fail to show up.
- **Pitch Angle**: *"We stop no-shows by collecting a 20% deposit upfront via card, and we automate your calendar so you never play phone tag again."*
- **Target Niches**:
  - Mobile Car Detailing ($150 - $400 ticket size)
  - House & Commercial Cleaning ($200 - $600 ticket size)
  - HVAC & Plumbing Service Calls ($99 dispatch fee)
  - Pet Grooming & Veterinary Mobile Care ($80 - $250 ticket size)
  - Window Tinting & Auto Wraps ($250 - $1,200 ticket size)

## 2. Pricing Models
- **Option 1: One-Time Setup + Handover**:
  - **Price**: $750 - $1,500 one-time fee.
  - Deliver custom branding, domain connection, and Stripe onboarding.
- **Option 2: Low Setup + Recurring Care Plan (Recommended)**:
  - **Price**: $399 setup + $49/month hosting, backup, and text alert maintenance.
  - 10 clients at $49/mo = $490/month in predictable recurring income.

## 3. High-Converting Cold Email / Instagram DM Script
> "Hey [Owner Name], noticed your work on [Instagram/Google/Facebook] - looks amazing! Quick question: are you currently taking bookings manually through DM or over the phone?
>
> I built a modern mobile booking system specifically for [Business Type] that lets your clients pick their package, choose an open time slot on your schedule, and pay a deposit upfront so they don't flake.
>
> I put together a quick 60-second interactive demo for your business here: [YOUR_PORTFOLIO_LINK]. Take a look and let me know if you'd like me to hook this up for your website this week!"
