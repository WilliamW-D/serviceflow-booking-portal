# 💰 ServiceFlow Client Acquisition & Monetization Guide

This guide gives you the exact blueprint to start reaching out to business owners and closing deals using your live ServiceFlow demo.

---

## 🎯 Step 1: Find 10 High-Probability Target Businesses (30 Minutes)

Open Google Maps or Instagram and search for local businesses in your city:
- **Mobile Car Detailers** (Keywords: *"Mobile detailing [your city]"*)
- **Pressure Washing / Roof Cleaning** (Keywords: *"Pressure washing [your city]"*)
- **Pet Grooming Services** (Keywords: *"Mobile dog grooming [your city]"*)
- **House Cleaning / Maid Services** (Keywords: *"House cleaning [your city]"*)
- **Window Tinting / Vehicle Wrap Shops** (Keywords: *"Window tint [your city]"*)

Look for businesses that:
1. Have good reviews (so they have active customers).
2. Currently say: *"Call or text (555) 123-4567 to book"* or *"DM us for an estimate"*.
3. Do **NOT** have an instant online booking system that collects deposits.

---

## 📱 Step 2: Send the 60-Second Cold Pitch

Send this via **Instagram DM**, **Facebook Messenger**, or **Email**:

### Script:
> **Subject:** Quick question about your booking setup for [Business Name]
>
> "Hey [Owner's First Name],
>
> Love your recent work on [mention specific job/car/house you saw on their page] — looks super clean!
>
> Quick question: are you still booking all your appointments manually through DMs or over the phone?
>
> I built a modern mobile booking portal specifically for [detailing / cleaning / grooming] pros that lets your customers pick their package, choose an open time on your schedule, and pay a 20% deposit upfront via card so you never get ghosted by no-shows.
>
> I set up a quick interactive demo you can test right on your phone:
> 👉 **[Insert your live demo URL here]**
>
> If you like how it works, I can brand it with your logo, put in your custom packages, and connect your Stripe account this week.
>
> No pressure at all — take a peek and let me know what you think!"

---

## 💡 Step 3: Handling the Response & Nailing the Price

When they reply asking *"How much does this cost?"*, respond with this simple two-option menu:

### Pricing Pitch:
> "We offer two simple options depending on what works best for your cash flow:
>
> **Option 1: One-Time Custom Setup ($650)**
> - We customize the portal with your branding, colors, and exact packages.
> - We set up the calendar with your working hours and block off your days off.
> - We connect your Stripe account so customer deposits go directly into your bank.
> - Zero recurring monthly fee to us — you own the code.
>
> **Option 2: Low Setup + Care Plan ($299 setup + $49/month)**
> - We do the full custom setup for just $299 upfront.
> - For $49/month, we handle all cloud hosting, ongoing text alert maintenance, and update your menu/prices whenever you want.
>
> Which of those sounds like a better fit for you?"

---

## 📈 Step 4: Show Them the ROI (Closing the Deal)

If they hesitate on price, refer directly to the **Sales & Pitch Kit** inside the app:
- *"If you do 35 jobs a month at an average of $250, and just 3 or 4 customers flake or cancel on you, you're losing over $10,000 every single year.*
- *By collecting a 20% deposit, customer flakes drop by over 80%. This system pays for itself in your first 2 to 3 weeks."*

---

## 🚀 Step 5: How to Deliver the Customization (Under 1 Hour)
When a client pays you:
1. Change the business name in `index.html` and `app.js` to their business name.
2. In `app.js`, edit `DEFAULT_SERVICES` to match their actual service list and pricing.
3. Replace the logo / colors in `styles.css`.
4. Deploy to their domain (e.g. via Vercel or Netlify) and hand them their admin credentials.
5. Collect payment and ask for a testimonial!
